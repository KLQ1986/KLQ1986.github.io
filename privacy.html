CyberX privacy policy

CyberX is used by its owner alone, on the owner's own computer.

What it reads

With the owner's consent it reads and labels the owner's Gmail, uploads videos to the owner's YouTube channel, manages their captions, and reads that channel's statistics and analytics.

Where the data stays

Data from Google APIs is kept only on the owner's computer. Tokens are kept in the macOS keychain. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

CyberX's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
